Voice for messages

Do

  • Say what happened in plain words.
  • Say if anything changed. Keep "Nothing was changed".
  • Give the next step as a button.
  • Take the blame for system problems: "on my end".

Don't

  • Blame the person or use codes (401, 503).
  • Joke about safety, gas, power or water.
  • Use exclamation marks in errors.
  • Hide a failed save behind a green tick.
